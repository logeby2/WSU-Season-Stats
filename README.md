# WSU Practice Stats 26-27

Full season, full fall, current week and the most recent practice days - box, advanced, shooting (contested / open),
defense, hard hats and rebounding - from the Synergy practice tags.

- `index.html` - the app (one file, no dependencies)
- `data.json` - the practice data, encrypted with the team passcode

Open the site and enter the passcode.

## Adding a practice

1. Open the site, go to **Admin**, and choose the Synergy CSV for one practice.
2. Check its name and date, fix anything it flags, then **Add this practice**.
3. In Admin, click **Download data.json**.
4. Here on GitHub: **Add file > Upload files**, drop in `data.json` (it replaces the old one), and **Commit changes**.
